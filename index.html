Enterprise Migration from Excel Databases and Dashboards to SQL and Power BI for a Global Office Supply Company

Deskify Office Supply Company’s relied on Excel to compile, analyse, and report monthly sales and transaction data. As transaction volumes grew, the process became increasingly time-consuming and inefficient. To address this, we developed a centralized data model using the company’s sales and transaction reporting database with T-SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Fabric. The solution consolidated data sources, automated data cleaning, and reduced manual processes, improving reporting accuracy and efficiency by 60%.
